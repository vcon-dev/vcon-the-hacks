# VCONIC TADHack 2026 — Winners

> **Event:** [VCONIC TADHack](https://blog.tadhack.com/2025/12/19/vconic-tadhack/) | March 7-8, 2026 | Virtual
>
> **Judge:** Thomas McCarthy-Howe | **Host:** Alan Quayle ([TADHack](https://tadhack.com))

The VCONIC TADHack 2026 drew 16 submissions from developers across four continents. The quality of the work was exceptional — every submission demonstrated deep understanding of the vCon standard and its real-world applications. Winners were selected across two tracks: a **Senior/Professional Track** recognizing innovation and community leadership, and a **Student Track** with cash prizes for emerging developers.

See **[HACKATHON-REVIEW.md](HACKATHON-REVIEW.md)** for full reviews of all submissions.

---

## Senior / Professional Track

*These awards recognize innovation, leadership, and community impact from experienced practitioners. No cash prizes — respect is the currency.*

---

### Senior Class Winner — Apparitions

**Team:** David Sikes & Jared Ashcraft | [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kYvfCVWyz1M) | [vCon](vcons/apparitions.vcon.json)

A location-based augmented reality framework where each point of interest is a vCon. Users walk through physical spaces — museums, historical sites, mystery stories — and audio/media triggers as they approach GPS coordinates stored in vCon attachments.

> *"When I first saw that hack, I was on the way over to China and I put the laptop down. I shut the laptop because my head was spinning... I hadn't thought of this. To be able to take a vCon as a dialogue, as a piece of data — to power museum experiences, product experiences, real-time experiences — and such a small piece of integration. No APIs. Wow."*
>
> *"I had thought of conversations as being assets, and man did they really explain to the world how assets are used."*

This is not David and Jared's first time bringing this level of vision to TADHack. Their consistent track record of making the community think differently about what conversations can be — and what they can power — has made them invaluable voices in this space.

---

### Matriculation Award — Ollie

**Team:** Anna Correa | [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=guBpk1E9yZA) | [vCon](vcons/ollie.vcon.json)

An AI-powered platform to help find and rescue lost animals faster through coordinated conversation tracking.

> *"Anna is not acting like a student. The creativity, the execution, the understanding of what vCons do and why they do it, and just the technical sense — she's no longer allowed to get the student discount. And you know, we've seen some real brilliant people come up through the TADHack community. She's one to watch."*

The Matriculation Award recognizes a student participant who has demonstrably crossed the threshold into professional-grade work. Anna is no longer competing in the student track.

---

### Senior Respect Mention — vCon Example App & vCon Intelligence Platform

**Team:** Muntaser Syed | [Video (Example App)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=msvvAcZFEng) | [Video (Intelligence Platform)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h4ehOvFSqLw) | [vCon (Example App)](vcons/vcon-example-app.vcon.json) | [vCon (Intelligence Platform)](vcons/vcon-intelligence-platform.vcon.json)

A reference implementation demonstrating vCon JSON-LD extensions, cryptographic signing, and MongoDB vector search — plus a comprehensive multi-backend platform for conversation intelligence.

> *"What an incredible intellect. What great work. Muntaser did the MCP extension into vCon, and that's a PR we're testing right now to pull in — that's going to be part of the MCP server forever. This is a block of code this community will use and value. I want to thank him for really having the foresight to do it for us, and also to show us how it's done. That guy's got skills."*

Muntaser submitted two high-quality hacks and contributed a production-ready MCP extension that is actively being merged into the vCon MCP Server.

## Student Track

*Cash prizes for emerging developers making their names in the TADHack community.*

---

### Grand Prize — vCohort ($3,000)

**Team:** Ziyad Shuaibu, Abdulalim Ladan, Mubarak Ibrahim | [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j6f88p8DIZU) | [vCon](vcons/vcohort.vcon.json)

An educational platform supporting bootcamps and cohort-based learning in Nigeria, using vCon to capture and structure educational conversations — enabling better tracking of student progress, mentor interactions, and learning outcomes.

> *"It really showed complete understanding of vCon. It's a piece of data, we're getting it from different places — and here's a real strong business case. We could use that all the time. If you don't use these social techniques in the future, what are you doing?"*
>
> *"The problem with communication is assuming it happened. What vCohort allows you to do is to teach and then to validate — did the communication really happen? Where are the spots? Did it get through? Can I close the loop. That was just so foundational for so many things."*
>
> *"Anyone using this is probably better than anyone else in the market at the moment, because they're not using conversations like these guys are."*

Ziyad, Abdulalim, and Mubarak have been involved in TADHack for a number of years, and it's great to see them achieving such success.

---

### Honorable Mention — Community Watch ($1,000)

**Team:** Victor Abdul | [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7RWY4BdJNb8) | [vCon](vcons/community-watch.vcon.json)

Turns fragmented neighborhood safety reports into a unified, interoperable intelligence stream using the vCon data standard. Community sightings are structured, aggregated, and analyzed in real time by Groq AI — giving residents, NGOs, and first responders a shared picture of what's happening on the ground.

> *"I love that — in the same way as Apparitions or Ollie — I like that we're treating it like data. I like understanding this is just another piece of data, how do we use this data in interesting ways? I thought that was picked up really well."*
>
> *"I also like the human side of it. vCons have two sides — they protect people. And protecting people is more than just the protocol to us. It's really one of the reasons we do this."*

---

### Honorable Mention — Life Canvas ($1,000)

**Team:** Sabrina (Valencia College) | [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C0UhGJR72pk) | [vCon](vcons/life-canvas.vcon.json)

A personal life intelligence system that captures everyday moments and turns them into searchable, structured insights using vCon.

> *"Just neck and neck with Anna... amazing personal development work you can do with this, at a very high level of presentation. She plucks at the heartstrings."*
>
> *"I think there are two things to being a really effective CTO: one is to be good at technology, which Sabrina obviously communicates — but also you have to explain it to people who aren't. You're a bridge. You're a communicator. And Sabrina is a genius at that stuff. As a technical communicator she's just wonderful, and I think it showed up."*

Sabrina is the next matriculation candidate. Watch this space.
